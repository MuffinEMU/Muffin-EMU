# MuffinEMU Game Compatibility

This page lists games that have been reported to work in MuffinEMU. Compatibility can vary between devices, so a game that works on one device is not guaranteed to work on every device.

## Compatible Games

The following titles have been reported and verified working on at least one device:

* **New Super Mario Bros. U**
* **Super Mario 3D World**
* **Mario Kart 8**
* **Splatoon** (base game)
* **Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker**
* **The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD**

> **Please note:** This list does not guarantee perfect performance or full gameplay compatibility. Performance, graphics, stability, and overall playability may vary depending on your device and MuffinEMU version.

## Contributing

Have another game working in MuffinEMU, or tested one of these games on a different device?

**Help expand the compatibility list!** Open a [GitHub issue](../../issues) with the following information:

* **Game:** Title and region/version, if known.
* **Device:** Your iPhone or iPad model.
* **MuffinEMU version:** The version or build you tested.
* **Results:** Does the game boot, reach gameplay, and run reliably?
* **Notes:** Any graphical issues, crashes, performance problems, or other observations.

Reports from different devices help build a more accurate picture of MuffinEMU's compatibility. Please share what you've tested, including any limitations you encountered.
