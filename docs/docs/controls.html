Controls & GamePad

A measured on-screen Wii U GamePad, a hide-controls button, comfort controls, and an alternative pad if you would rather use it.

On this page

  • The on-screen GamePad
  • Melo-Controller
  • Controller skins

The on-screen GamePad

The pad's proportions are measured, not designed: taken from the official Wii U GamePad's own front illustration at 0.425 mm per pixel, a scale checked twice against Nintendo's published dimensions and confirmed both times — body 255.0 mm measured against 255 mm official, screen 158 mm diagonal measured against 157.5 mm official.

Every dimension is expressed as a multiple of one face-button diameter, and the body works out to exactly 24 of them wide — so "life-size" on a large-enough screen is a literal claim, not a figure of speech. See the full measurement breakdown in the GamePad layout reference.

Hide controls

A button in the in-game top bar toggles the on-screen pad's visibility without pausing — useful the moment you need to touch the actual GamePad screen underneath it (a keyboard, a map, anything the game draws there) instead of the buttons layered over it. Hiding it also releases every currently-held button, so nothing stays "stuck" pressed once the overlay is gone.

Comfort controls

An optional layout for the analog sticks: with comfort controls on, L/ZL/minus sit on the left stick and R/ZR/plus sit on the right stick, instead of staying on the d-pad and A/B/X/Y. Only available once the analog sticks toggle is on — moving shoulder buttons onto a stick that is not there does nothing.

Sizing, feel, and layout

  • Button size and opacity sliders — MuffinEMU already picks a sensible default for your screen; the sliders adjust that choice rather than replace it.
  • Add analog sticks — shown alongside the d-pad and face buttons, not instead of them.
  • Stick gate (the shape the stick is allowed to move within), plus separate deadzone and response curve controls for how that shape is read.
  • Haptic feedback — a light tap on press; turn it off if it reads as buzzing rather than a button.
  • Reset layout — puts every on-screen control setting back to its shipped default.

Melo-Controller

Melo-Controller, stossy11's own touch controller from MeloCafe, is available as a full alternative to MuffinEMU's measured pad — toggle "Use melo-controls" in Settings > On-screen Controls. It brings its own button art, its own layout editor for repositioning and resizing individual buttons, and its own per-game layouts. A separate scale slider resizes the whole pad as one unit on top of whatever the layout editor has already done to individual buttons.

Licensing

Melo-Controller is GPL-3.0 and is linked into every MuffinEMU build whether or not the toggle is on. Because GPL-3.0 code is present in the binary, a MuffinEMU IPA as a whole is distributed under GPL-3.0 — see Credits & license.

Controller skins

20 visual skins for the on-screen pad, independent of layout: Standard, Wii U Original, GameCube, Nintendo 64, Super Nintendo, NES, Switch Pro, PlayStation, Xbox, Steam Deck, Arcade Cabinet, Sega Genesis, Minimal, Glass, Neon, Dark Mode, Light Mode, Custom, Mario Theme, and Zelda Theme.
